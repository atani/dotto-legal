Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Dotto ("the App") is a minimalist task manager for iOS developed by an independent developer.

Data Collection

Dotto does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All tasks, reminders, and settings are stored locally on your device.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync (Pro feature), your data is stored in your private iCloud container managed by Apple. The developer has no access to this data. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud storage: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third-Party Services

Dotto does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive any payment information. Apple's terms apply.

Children's Privacy

Dotto does not knowingly collect data from children. Since no data is collected from anyone, the App is safe for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue at github.com/atani/dotto-legal/issues.
