Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Tap the ⋯ menu at the top right, then select Upgrade to Pro. The upgrade is a one-time purchase — no subscription.

How do I set a reminder?

Tap on a task to open it, then tap the bell icon to pick a date and time. You will receive a notification at that time. Free users can set up to 3 reminders per day; Pro users have no limit.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchase at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. Pro users can enable iCloud sync to keep tasks in sync across iPhone and iPad. Your data is stored in your private iCloud container — only you can access it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are managed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
